Register a text

The contract stores the text, its title and the URL where it was published. The block time is the first-publication record. Anyone can later point at another page and ask validators how much of it is this text.

Works

Newest first. Attesting a page costs a consensus round; the contract stores the category, the overlap bucket, the score it computes, and a short verbatim excerpt.

How the reading works

  1. Validators fetch the suspect page and read it against the registered text.
  2. They answer one category (verbatim, near copy, derivative, unrelated) and one overlap bucket (none, low, medium, high, full).
  3. The round only settles when both agree on those two values; wording and excerpt are free.
  4. The contract maps the pair to a score and a status: 100 verbatim, 85 near copy, 50 review, 0 clear.

What it is not

A registration is evidence, not a copyright, and a status is a computed label, not a legal finding. It records that this text existed at this time and how a validator set read that page.
