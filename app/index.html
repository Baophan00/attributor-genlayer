How the reading works

  1. Validators fetch the suspect page and read it against the registered text.
  2. They answer one category and one overlap bucket.
  3. The round settles only when both agree; wording and excerpt are free.
  4. The contract maps the pair to a score and a status.

First publication, then dispute

  1. Publish the text and the owner proof line on the page.
  2. Registration fetches that page and verifies both before writing.
  3. A different address can challenge with an earlier page; one round can revoke the record.
  4. The owner can claim a near-copy or verbatim finding.
