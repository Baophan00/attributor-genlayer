Why translation memory keeps losing your brand voice

Published 4 November 2026 · demo page for Attributor

Every translation memory fails quietly: it keeps the words and loses the decisions. A glossary is exactly that kind of decision. The product is a payment gateway, never a payment door, in each sentence, permanently. If the memory stores only the sentence and forgets the decision, the next translator produces a smooth page the brand cannot release, and the check lands at midnight, right before launch.
