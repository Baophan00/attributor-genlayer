Why glossaries fail at midnight

Published 12 October 2026 · demo page for Attributor

The quiet failure of every translation memory is that it remembers words and forgets decisions. A glossary is a decision: this product is called a payment gateway, not a payment door, in every sentence, forever. When the memory only keeps the sentence and drops the decision, the next translator writes a fluent page that the brand cannot ship, and the review happens at midnight before launch.
